## Target and specificity contract

Plotting glyphs by degree, drawing aspects, choosing house conventions, reindexing counts, and comparing overlaid wheels connect every course concept.

Longitude, Cartesian marker position, sign count, house count, and quadrant histogram remain distinct. Named chart actions are interpreted geometrically, not as demonstrated personality or causal models.

Each entry needs an actual event, action, or observed record; a before/after or
measured quantity; an exact mathematical landing; an explicit limitation; a
callback; and a fresh ordinary mathematics question. Renaming generic variables
with domain nouns is insufficient. Every taught concept, including supplements,
must have a registered destination. A named object need not represent a theorem
if its useful role is an honest boundary or initial-condition callback.

## Domain-specific rejection tests

1. Signs and houses are conflated; whole-sign boundaries are equated with the Ascendant degree, or equal/Placidus houses are modeled as the same permutation.
2. A square aspect is called a square matrix, an opposition is called orthogonal, or a favorable interpretation is identified with positive covariance.
3. Raw angles are averaged across 0 degrees without a circular-data rule; zero resultant is normalized into a direction.
4. A coordinate/display change is presented as actual planetary motion, or a real rotation is assumed to have a real eigenbasis.
5. PCA or a matrix fit is claimed to validate astrological personality/forecast interpretations; invented placements are called real chart data.
6. A course concept lacks an explicit mechanism anchor and mathematical landing,
   or the graph connects topics only through shared imagery.
7. Dimensions, assumptions, singular/zero cases, normalization, or computations
   are wrong; a metaphor takes the place of proof.
8. Drafted examples are called empirically tested, chapter-integrated, or effective
   with learners without evidence. Real/signed extensions are passed off as literal
   count or material instructions.

Any unresolved rejection prevents a claim of full readiness regardless of score.
Do not mix this canon's entities with another analogy midway through an explanation.
Cross-analogy comparisons must explicitly identify the shared mathematics.

## Review procedure

1. Walk this exact route: **PLACE → ASPECT/ROTATE → HOUSES/COUNT → SPACE/FIT/MODES → CENTER/SURVEY**. Follow the rest of the graph too.
2. Compare every taught mathematical concept against the coverage register and
   exact course sources. Check explicit chapter locations rather than assuming
   that a drafted table means the concept is taught.
3. Apply the rejection tests and recalculate the numerical anchors, including
   dimensions, null directions, projections, eigen/SVD relationships, and statistics.
4. Verify domain claims against linked authoritative or practitioner sources.
   Separate a source-backed fact from an invented mathematical model or dataset.
5. Inspect diagrams and accessible descriptions. Give fresh formal problems and
   record what a reader can do independently and what still causes confusion.
6. Replace only Latest assessment after meaningful work. Changing target/weights
   establishes a new baseline; do not claim progress from incomparable scores.
