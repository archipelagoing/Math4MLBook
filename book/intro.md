# Mathematics, stitch by stitch

Concise course guides retaining the lecture definitions, formulas, examples, and exam
checks. Knitting and crochet keep the ideas connected; each example states what
the mathematical record represents.

:::{admonition} Start here · Lecture 1 in two reading pages
:class: key-result

1. [Vectors, combinations, lengths, and angles](lecture1/index.md).
2. [Matrices, multiplication, and common exam traps](lecture1/matrix-guide.md).
:::

Study the yellow results, reproduce the examples, then answer the self-checks
without looking. Red panels flag mistakes that change an answer.

Lecture 1 summarizes Erisa Terolli's CS 556 Week 1 slides supplied in `resources/`.
The stitch examples are added teaching constructions. Later lecture guides are
not yet available.
