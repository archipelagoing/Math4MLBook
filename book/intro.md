# Mathematics, in view

A textbook for understanding the mathematics behind the course: connected
explanations, precise formulas, diagrams, worked examples, and practice.
Marked points and their shadows provide the recurring scene. Each coordinate
record states what a view reveals and what it leaves out.

:::{admonition} Lecture 1 · Vectors and matrices
:class: key-result

1. [Vectors and geometry](lecture1/index.md) — ordered records, combinations, dot products, lengths, and angles.
2. [Matrices and products](lecture1/matrix-guide.md) — matrix structure, arithmetic, transpose, trace, and multiplication.
3. [Exercises](lecture1/practice.md) — 24 questions from foundations to synthesis.
4. [Solutions](lecture1/solutions.md) — explanations, checks, and targeted revision.
:::

:::{admonition} Lecture 2 · Systems of linear equations

3. [Exercises](lecture2/practice.md) — 18 questions from foundations to synthesis.
4. [Solutions](lecture2/solutions.md) — explained steps, checks, and targeted revision.

Each lecture uses at most **four reading pages, including exercises and solutions**.
Section headings and the contents panel help you navigate within a page.

Pink definitions introduce the language, green examples show it in use, yellow
results highlight what to retain, and red pitfalls flag common mistakes. Read an
example, cover its solution, and reproduce the reasoning before trying a new problem.
