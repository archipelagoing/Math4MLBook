## Editorial additions and clarifications

- Explicit m×n dimensions, augmented notation, a six-step solving workflow, and
  reversible-operation justification. Scaling requires a **nonzero** constant.
- Standard stage distinction: Gaussian forward elimination to REF followed by
  back substitution; Gauss–Jordan continues to RREF. The slides use Gaussian
  elimination broadly for the entire reduction. Their result is preserved.
- Pivot/free-variable and consistency explanations, including multiple parameters,
  row swaps, and unique solutions despite redundant rows. These prepare later rank
  material without implying that it has already been taught.
- The p. 8 matrix has **no augmentation bar**. Example 2.5 treats it as an ordinary
  matrix; Problem 10 explicitly declares an augmented interpretation before using it.
- Canonical front view O and stacked view H, repeated Y constraints, hidden depth,
  and conflicting exact measurements. Row operations rewrite equations; they do
  not move the point or create independent observations. No averaging is substituted
  for solving the exact equations.
- Three original SVG diagrams, eight checkpoints, 18 graduated exercises with
  hints and explained solutions, revision links and an unaided readiness map.
